Who we are

Two doctors under one roof in 4th Block, Rajajinagar

Raghav Dental Care Centre is a two-doctor clinic at 298, 10th Main Road, 4th Block, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560010, opposite Canara Bank. Dr. Raghavendra K is an Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon, Implantologist and Cosmetic/Aesthetic Dentist, in practice since 1997. Dr. Swetha Raghavendra is a Dental Surgeon in restorative, cosmetic and family dentistry, in practice since 2002.

Between them, the clinic covers both specialist and everyday care. Dr. Raghavendra K's work includes dental implants, wisdom-tooth and surgical extraction, oral and maxillofacial trauma and pathology, jaw correction and smile rehabilitation. Dr. Swetha Raghavendra looks after crowns and bridges, root canal and re-root canal treatment, fillings, children's dentistry, aligners, cosmetic dentistry and full-mouth rehabilitation.

That combination is the point of the clinic: a family can have check-ups, fillings and a child's first visit in the same place where a wisdom tooth is removed or an implant is planned. Where a case needs an orthodontist, periodontist or paediatric dentist, our dentists assess you first and involve or refer to a specialist when your case needs it. We consult in English and Kannada.

Visit to visit

How we practise

Five habits you can expect at every appointment, whichever of the two doctors you see.

Explanations first

We begin by explaining what we have found and why, in plain language, in English or Kannada. Questions are welcome at every visit, not only the first one.

Options discussed, plan shared before we proceed

Where there is more than one way to treat a problem, we talk you through each one. You see the treatment plan before any work begins.

Calm, gentle, pain-minimised care

If you are anxious about dental treatment, tell us. We work at a steady pace, explain each step as it comes and keep discomfort as low as we can.

Instruments sterilised between patients

Instruments are sterilised between patients. If you would like to know how we do it, ask us at your visit.

Follow-up discussed before you leave

Before you leave, we go through aftercare and whether a review visit is needed. If something does not feel right afterwards, call the clinic during clinic hours and we will advise you.

Plain speaking

Our commitment to honest information

We do not use superlatives on this website, and we do not promise results. Whether a treatment suits you, how many visits it takes and what outcome to expect vary from person to person, and are decided only after a clinical examination.

What you read here is educational. It does not replace an examination, a diagnosis or advice given to you in person. If something on this site raises a question, bring it to your appointment and we will answer it plainly.

Who we serve

Who we see, and where they come from

We are a neighbourhood clinic first, for families and working adults in Rajajinagar, Basaveshwaranagar, Mahalakshmi Layout, Malleswaram and nearby areas: check-ups, cleaning, fillings, root canals, crowns and children's dentistry.

We also see patients from across Bengaluru who need dental implants, wisdom-tooth removal or an oral and maxillofacial opinion. Specialist referrals are welcome.

We see seniors who need restorative work, children at their first visits, and adults who have put off treatment because they are nervous. Whoever you are, you will be told what we found, what the options are and what happens next.

The clinic is open every day, including Sunday mornings. Appointments are by WhatsApp or phone; the contact page has hours and directions. Cash, cards and UPI are accepted.

Questions

About the clinic

Who are the doctors at Raghav Dental Care Centre?

Two doctors practise here. Dr. Raghavendra K is an Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon, Implantologist and Cosmetic/Aesthetic Dentist, in practice since 1997, with a focus on implants, wisdom-tooth and surgical extraction, oral and maxillofacial trauma and pathology, jaw correction and smile rehabilitation. Dr. Swetha Raghavendra is a Dental Surgeon, in practice since 2002, with a focus on crowns and bridges, root canal and re-root canal treatment, fillings, children's dentistry, aligners, cosmetic dentistry and full-mouth rehabilitation. Their full profiles are on the Doctors page.

Is this a specialist clinic or a family clinic?

Both. Routine family dentistry (check-ups, cleaning, fillings, root canals, crowns and children's visits) and specialist work (dental implants, wisdom-tooth removal and oral and maxillofacial surgery) are available through the same clinic. Where a case needs an orthodontist, periodontist or paediatric dentist, our dentists assess you first and involve or refer to a specialist when your case needs it.

I am anxious about dental treatment. What should I expect?

Tell us when you book or when you arrive. We explain what we have found before anything else, go through the options with you, and work at a steady pace, explaining each step as it comes. We aim for calm, gentle, pain-minimised care: we do not claim treatment is free of all sensation, but we will keep it as comfortable as we can and answer every question you have.

Which areas do you serve, and do you consult in Kannada?

We serve Rajajinagar, Basaveshwaranagar, Mahalakshmi Layout, Malleswaram and nearby areas, and see implant, wisdom-tooth and maxillofacial patients from across Bengaluru. We consult in English and Kannada. The clinic is open every day, including Sunday mornings; book on WhatsApp or call the clinic to request an appointment.
